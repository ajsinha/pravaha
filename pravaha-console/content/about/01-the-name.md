## pravāha — that which flows

In Sanskrit *pravāha* is the **current**: a river's flow, continuous and
directional. Not the water, which is always different water — the *flowing*,
which is the thing that persists.

Which is what a stream is, and what makes streaming hard to reason about. The
rows are gone the moment they pass. What you keep is the answer they changed,
and the discipline is knowing which answers a row can still change and which it
cannot.

That question — *can anything still arrive that changes this?* — is the one that
watermarks answer, that retention rests on, and that decides when a window may
close. Nearly every subtle bug in a streaming system is a place where somebody
assumed the answer was "no" and it was "yes".
