# press-02 reads 720/724 in the first minute, climbs 780 + 25n in the second, and 940 + 8n in the
# third. press-02-late (08:02:55, 991) arrives after the third minute closed but inside thirty
# seconds of allowed lateness, so the minute is corrected to seven readings and a maximum of 991.

2026-03-02T08:02:00Z | 6 | 780 | 905 | 28
