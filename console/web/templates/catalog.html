Sinks cannot be listed yet

A sink is bound in the engine's configuration under pravaha.sinks.<name> and named at registration. The engine publishes no API that lists those bindings, their plugin, their row shape, whether they accept retractions, or which queries write to them.

Needed: GET /api/v1/sinks → [{name, plugin, fields:[{name,type}], keyColumns, acceptsRetractions, writers:[query]}] and a sink field on each entry of the pravaha.list action. Until then the workbench's register panel takes a sink by name, and the engine checks it (PRV-8010, PRV-2041).
