## Decision

Adapt performance automatically; never adapt semantics

## Alternatives considered

Full manual tuning; adapt everything

## Rationale and consequences

Operations is where streaming projects die, but silent semantic changes are unacceptable. Every controller is observable, bounded, reversible and pinnable (§18.1)
