## Decision

## Alternatives considered

Apache 2.0 (proposed in an earlier revision of this document); open core; source-available; dual licence

## Rationale and consequences

Owner's decision. The technical moat in §2 is unaffected, but the distribution risk moves from execution to evaluation: §30.4 sets out what has to carry the weight instead — published Nexmark results, a frictionless evaluation licence, and the developer-experience surface
